# What HYPD can do

HYPD analyses the user's real advertising and analytics data, read-only: it never changes campaigns, budgets or ads.

**Ad platforms:** Google Ads · Meta (Facebook) Ads and the Meta Ad Library · Microsoft Ads · LinkedIn Ads.

**Analytics and shopping:** Google Analytics (GA4) · Search Console · Merchant Center.

**Research:** keyword ideas with volumes and CPCs, SERP and competitor ads, Shopping sellers, and technical landing-page checks on a live URL.

**Typical questions:**

- "Audit my Google Ads account and rank the issues by impact."
- "Where am I wasting spend, and how much?"
- "Which search terms should become negative keywords?"
- "Why did performance drop last week?"
- "Build a monthly report for my client."
- "Which Merchant Center products are disapproved, and why?"
- "What is going wrong in my Meta account?"
- "How are my LinkedIn and Microsoft Ads campaigns doing?"
- "Which pages lost organic clicks in Search Console?"
- "What ads are my competitors running?"

For ready-made analyses, use the **hypd-workflows** skill. Anything else: ask in plain language.
